### 1. The 3 Primary Facebook Ads Audience Categories
* **Cold Audience:** Yeh woh log hote hain jo aapke business, brand, ya offers ke baare mein kuch bhi nahi jaante (Zero Awareness); inka potential size sabse bada hota hai[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span).
* **Warm Audience:** Yeh log aapke brand ya product se pehle se familiar hote hain, jinhone aapki website visit ki hai ya Instagram/Facebook page par engage kiya hai[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).
* **Hot Audience:** Yeh aapke existing customers hote hain jo pehle hi aapse koi na koi product ya service khareed chuke hain aur inka trust level maximum hota hai[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

### 2. Deep-Dive into Cold Audience & Conversion Economics
* **Targeting via Saved Audience (Core Audience):** Location (jaise Patna, Bihar), demographics (age, gender), aur detailed targeting (interests & behaviors) ke zariye cold audience target ki jaati hai[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
* **Conversion Math:** Cold audience ki conversion rate low (0.2% - 0.5%) hoti hai aur Cost Per Acquisition (CAC) high hoti hai kyunki brand trust zero hota hai[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).

### 3. Deep-Dive into Warm Audience & Retargeting Strategy
* **Touchpoints:** Website visitors (via Meta Pixel), social media engagers, aur video viewers jo content ke 50% ya 75% hisse ko dekh chuke hain[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
* **Retargeting & ROI:** Warm audience yield 5x more conversions & 80% lower CAC compared to cold audience[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span). Meta Ads mein custom audience banakar retargeting ki jaati hai[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).

### 4. Hot Audience, Tripwire Strategy, Cross-Selling & Up-Selling
* **Tripwire Strategy:** Ek low-cost, high-value product (jaise Rs. 149 mini-course) ke zariye cold/warm strangers ko hot paying customers mein convert kiya jata hai[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
* **Cross-Selling vs. Up-Selling:**
